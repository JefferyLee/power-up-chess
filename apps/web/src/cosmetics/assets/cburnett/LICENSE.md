# Cburnett piece set

"cburnett" piece set, drawn by Colin M.L. Burnett and originally published
on Wikipedia under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 Unported
license (also released under 2.5 Generic, 2.0 Generic, and 1.0 Generic).
