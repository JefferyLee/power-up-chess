SAFE BY DESIGN

  • · No personal info. Display name and a 4-character magic word. No email, no phone, no real name.
  • · No DMs, no public matchmaking. Online play is private-room-link only — kids only play with people they invited or were invited by.
  • · Server-moderated chat. Profanity filter, rate limits, report flag, auto-hide.
  • · Honest praise only. "Brilliant / Excellent / Mistake / Blunder" comes from Stockfish analysis, not templates — your kid never gets fake compliments.

BUILT BY A DAD FOR HIS DAUGHTER

Power Up Castle started because Ada wanted to play chess online with her friends — but every "chess for kids" site I found leaked personal info, public chat, ads, or all three.

So I built the castle she'd actually want to live in. Every feature you see here is something Ada (or one of her test players) asked for at the kitchen table.
