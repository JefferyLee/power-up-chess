WHAT'S INSIDE

Learn

Chess Basics lessons, endgame drills, an opening trainer, and Knight's Hop — short interactive steps from zero.

Solve

5,300+ adaptive puzzles across six themed plots, with a daily Today's Five quest and kid-friendly explanations.

Play

Invite friends by private link, join the weekly tournament, or face a practice partner that adapts to every win and loss.

Review

Every game ends with real Stockfish analysis — Best to Blunder, honestly labeled — and Lucy & Luca retell it as a story.

Masters Library

Replay the classics and 9,400+ grandmaster games — Morphy to Gukesh — with engine commentary on a fullscreen 3D board.

Explore

Side quests: Forest Adventure, Wizard's Duel, Knight's Run, Tower Defense, and a 108-story chess bookshelf.

Collect

Piece sets, board themes, daily streaks — all tracked on your Adventurer's Plaque.

Chat

The Great Hall: moderated lobby chat. Find any player, peek at their plaque, no DMs.

SAFE BY DESIGN

  • · No personal info. Display name and a 4-character magic word. No email, no phone, no real name.
  • · No DMs, no public matchmaking. Online play is private-room-link only — kids only play with people they invited or were invited by.
  • · Server-moderated chat. Profanity filter, rate limits, report flag, auto-hide.
  • · Honest praise only. "Brilliant / Excellent / Mistake / Blunder" comes from Stockfish analysis, not templates — your kid never gets fake compliments.
  • · AI on a leash. Host commentary runs server-side with the strictest safety filters — and a settings toggle turns AI-generated text off entirely.
  • · Leave without a trace. One tap on "forget me" erases everything, server and device. Leaderboards are opt-out too.

BUILT BY A DAD FOR HIS DAUGHTER

Power Up Castle started because Ada wanted chess to be more fun — a real castle for the kings, queens, and knights she was already moving around the board.

So the castle grew what she asked for at the kitchen table: Wizard's Duel, where chess pieces cast spells; a masters library to replay her hero Magnus Carlsen's games; puzzle plots to conquer; and honest feedback on every single move — real analysis, never an empty compliment.
